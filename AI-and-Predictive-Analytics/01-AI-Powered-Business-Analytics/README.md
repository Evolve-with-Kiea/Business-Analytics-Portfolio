## AI-Assisted Sales Performance Analysis

**Difficulty:** Beginner  
**Suggested tools:** Excel, optional LLM  
**Status:** Planned

### Business Problem

Business stakeholders need reliable evidence to answer: **Which products, periods, and segments explain changes in sales?** Without consistent definitions and validation, an AI-generated answer may be misleading or difficult to reproduce.

### Key Business Questions

- What does the available data establish about this problem?
- Which metrics or patterns are most useful to stakeholders?
- How does a transparent baseline compare with the proposed AI approach?
- What uncertainty, data gaps, or operational risks affect the conclusions?

### Planned Data & KPIs

**Potential inputs:** Sales transactions, dates, products, units, revenue, returns.

Define data grain, metric numerators and denominators, reporting periods, units, missing-value rules, and any target labels before analysis. Select evaluation metrics appropriate to the task, rather than assuming every project needs a predictive model.

### Planned Analytical Methods

1. Define the business question and expected decision.
2. Prepare synthetic or authorized data and document provenance.
3. Build a simple, independently verifiable analytical baseline.
4. Apply the proposed methods using Excel, optional LLM where appropriate.
5. Test outputs against source data, reviewed examples, or held-out observations.
6. Record limitations, errors, and recommended next steps.

### Expected Deliverables

Validated Excel analysis, AI-assisted draft narrative, evidence-to-claim audit. Supporting files may include a dataset description, notebook or workbook, methodology, validation log, visualization, and project README. Only link files after creation.

### Quality & Risk Considerations

Check all generated numbers against source calculations; avoid unsupported causal claims. Use human review and avoid representing hypothetical outputs as real outcomes.

### Potential Business Application

If validated, the approach could inform future business analytics, financial operations, marketing reporting, or educational products. This is a possible application, not an existing service.

## Data Integrity & Professional Standards

- Use synthetic, public licensed, or explicitly authorized data.
- Protect sensitive information and review third-party AI data handling.
- Disclose AI assistance and distinguish calculations from generated prose.
- Document reproducible steps, assumptions, model metrics, and limitations.
- Verify every reported result before publication.
- Do not claim client work, released products, or completed models without evidence.
