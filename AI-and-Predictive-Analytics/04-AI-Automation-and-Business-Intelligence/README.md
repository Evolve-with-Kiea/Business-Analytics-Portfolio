# AI Automation & Business Intelligence

**Business Analytics Portfolio | AI & Predictive Analytics**

**Focus:** Repeatable KPI reporting, document processing, data quality, and controlled business workflows

**Status:** Planned — Learning & Portfolio Development

## Overview

This category will explore repeatable kpi reporting, document processing, data quality, and controlled business workflows through documented, reproducible projects. Projects are proposed learning and portfolio activities; no completed analyses, validated models, production applications, or client outcomes are claimed.

## Business Objectives

1. Translate a practical business question into measurable analytical objectives.
2. Select suitable data and define reporting periods and metrics.
3. Compare transparent baselines with AI-enabled approaches when useful.
4. Validate calculations and evaluate errors and uncertainty.
5. Communicate findings, limitations, and evidence-based recommendations.

## Automated KPI Reporting Assistant

**Difficulty:** Intermediate  
**Suggested tools:** Excel, Python, optional LLM API  
**Status:** Planned

### Business Problem

Business stakeholders need reliable evidence to answer: **How can weekly KPI summaries be produced more efficiently?** Without consistent definitions and validation, an AI-generated answer may be misleading or difficult to reproduce.

### Key Business Questions

- What does the available data establish about this problem?
- Which metrics or patterns are most useful to stakeholders?
- How does a transparent baseline compare with the proposed AI approach?
- What uncertainty, data gaps, or operational risks affect the conclusions?

### Planned Data & KPIs

**Potential inputs:** Validated KPI tables and reporting periods.

Define data grain, metric numerators and denominators, reporting periods, units, missing-value rules, and any target labels before analysis. Select evaluation metrics appropriate to the task, rather than assuming every project needs a predictive model.

### Planned Analytical Methods

1. Define the business question and expected decision.
2. Prepare synthetic or authorized data and document provenance.
3. Build a simple, independently verifiable analytical baseline.
4. Apply the proposed methods using Excel, Python, optional LLM API where appropriate.
5. Test outputs against source data, reviewed examples, or held-out observations.
6. Record limitations, errors, and recommended next steps.

### Expected Deliverables

Reproducible report generator and review checklist. Supporting files may include a dataset description, notebook or workbook, methodology, validation log, visualization, and project README. Only link files after creation.

### Quality & Risk Considerations

Require source traceability and human approval. Use human review and avoid representing hypothetical outputs as real outcomes.

### Potential Business Application

If validated, the approach could inform future business analytics, financial operations, marketing reporting, or educational products. This is a possible application, not an existing service.

## Data Integrity & Professional Standards

- Use synthetic, public licensed, or explicitly authorized data.
- Protect sensitive information and review third-party AI data handling.
- Disclose AI assistance and distinguish calculations from generated prose.
- Document reproducible steps, assumptions, model metrics, and limitations.
- Verify every reported result before publication.
- Do not claim client work, released products, or completed models without evidence.
