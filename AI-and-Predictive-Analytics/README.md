## Overview

This specialization explores practical uses of artificial intelligence, statistics, machine learning, and business intelligence to answer business questions. It builds on financial accounting experience and ongoing analytics education. Project ideas are future work, not verified results, deployed products, certifications, or client engagements.

The objective is to compare AI-enabled methods with transparent analytical baselines, evaluate accuracy, and communicate actionable insights with appropriate human oversight.

## Specialization Objectives

1. Apply AI to clearly defined business decisions.
2. Develop reproducible financial and marketing analysis.
3. Compare predictive methods with simple baselines.
4. Measure errors, uncertainty, and limitations.
5. Build privacy-conscious, evidence-linked reporting workflows.
6. Document verified work for a professional portfolio.

## Standard Workflow

Define business problem → identify authorized data → prepare and validate → establish non-AI baseline → develop AI/model method → evaluate against held-out data or reviewed examples → inspect errors and risks → interpret findings → document and publish only verified evidence.

## Responsible AI & Quality Standards

- Use synthetic, public licensed, or explicitly authorized datasets.
- Never upload confidential financial, customer, or employee records to third-party AI services without permission and safeguards.
- Separate calculated facts, model predictions, and AI-generated interpretations.
- Document prompts, model versions, data sources, assumptions, and limitations when applicable.
- Evaluate results using appropriate metrics, test sets, and human review.
- Check hallucinations, data leakage, bias, privacy, and reproducibility.
- Do not present proposed prototypes or example calculations as client outcomes.
