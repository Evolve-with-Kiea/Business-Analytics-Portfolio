## Before adding another dataset

- Record its owner/publisher, exact source, retrieval date, license or permission, reporting coverage, and row grain.
- Keep raw identifiers and financial records out of public work unless appropriately authorized and protected.
- Label synthetic data. Do not claim it represents actual company or client performance.
- Check that the data supports the proposed metric; do not substitute a different denominator or invent missing history.
- Cite only datasets actually used. A useful website is not automatically a project data source.
