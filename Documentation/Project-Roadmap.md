# Project Roadmap

These are future ideas consolidated from the original planning folders. They are not completed projects and have no claimed results. Create one dedicated project folder when work starts; do not duplicate one analysis under multiple subjects.

## Suggested next practice

1. Budget versus actual expenses in Excel.
2. Sales and product profitability in Excel.
3. Reproduce AR summaries in SQL.
4. Rebuild the AR dashboard in Power BI.

The sequence is a learning suggestion, not a schedule or a claim that work has begun.

## Original idea register

### Excel Projects

| Idea / context | Business or learning question |
|---|---|
| Business Performance | How can an organization use clearly defined KPIs, targets, historical trends, and operational data to evaluate performance, identify improvement opportunities, and monitor progress toward business goals? |
| Sales Dashboard | How can a business use Microsoft Excel to evaluate sales performance, identify revenue trends, compare products and regions, and monitor progress toward sales targets? |

### Financial Analytics

| Idea / context | Business or learning question |
|---|---|
| Expense Analysis | How can businesses use financial data to understand cost behavior, evaluate expense trends, identify budget variances, and make more informed decisions about resource allocation? |
| Financial Reporting | How can financial statements, ratios, and historical comparisons be used together to evaluate profitability, liquidity, financial position, cash flow, and overall business performance? |
| Revenue and Profitability | How can businesses analyze revenue, costs, profit margins, and break-even points to understand financial performance and identify opportunities for further investigation? |

### Marketing Analytics

| Idea / context | Business or learning question |
|---|---|
| Campaign Performance | How can businesses use marketing data to compare campaigns, evaluate conversion performance, identify acquisition costs, and determine which areas warrant further optimization? |
| Customer Analysis | How can businesses use customer and transaction data to understand acquisition, retention, purchasing behavior, segmentation, and long-term customer value? |
| Marketing ROI | How can businesses compare marketing expenditures with attributed revenue and contribution profit to evaluate financial performance and identify opportunities for further investigation? |
