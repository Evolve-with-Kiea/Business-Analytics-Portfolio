## Project Development Workflow

Each project will follow a consistent analytical development process:

**1. Define** — Identify the business problem, stakeholders, and intended decisions.

**2. Plan** — Establish project scope, required skills, data needs, and deliverables.

**3. Prepare** — Collect, inspect, clean, and organize appropriate datasets.

**4. Analyze** — Calculate KPIs, investigate trends, and answer business questions.

**5. Visualize** — Develop reports, charts, dashboards, or analytical tools.

**6. Validate** — Verify calculations, reconcile totals, and review assumptions.

**7. Interpret** — Translate analytical results into business insights.

**8. Document** — Record methodology, findings, limitations, and lessons learned.

**9. Publish** — Present verified work with supporting evidence.

**10. Improve** — Identify opportunities for additional analysis and technical development.
