## Overview

Executive Dashboards focuses on integrating financial, marketing, audience, and operational data into a centralized business intelligence reporting system.

Creators, influencers, entrepreneurs, and digital businesses often manage performance information across multiple platforms, including social media accounts, affiliate programs, e-commerce stores, sponsorship agreements, and financial records.

Although each platform provides useful information, reviewing these sources separately can make it difficult to understand overall business performance.

This portfolio category will explore how business intelligence tools can consolidate multiple datasets, establish consistent performance indicators, and communicate meaningful insights through interactive executive dashboards.

Drawing on my professional background in financial accounting and business operations, I plan to develop analytical solutions that connect revenue performance, marketing effectiveness, audience development, and business profitability.

As my technical skills advance, this specialization will demonstrate how Excel, SQL, Power BI, and data modeling techniques can support strategic decision-making.

### Business Problem

Creator-led businesses frequently generate income and operational activity across multiple platforms.

Financial performance may be tracked in spreadsheets or accounting systems, while marketing results are reported through social media dashboards, affiliate platforms, and e-commerce analytics.

This fragmented reporting environment can make it difficult to answer important business questions consistently.

For example, a creator may experience increasing audience engagement while profitability declines because of rising production expenses or lower commission earnings.

Similarly, a business may report growing sponsorship revenue without recognizing that outstanding invoices are affecting cash availability.

This project will explore how integrated business intelligence reporting can provide a more complete view of creator business performance.

### Key Business Questions

- How is the creator business performing overall?
- Which revenue streams contribute the most income?
- How do audience growth and engagement trends compare with revenue performance?
- Which brand partnerships generate the strongest financial contribution?
- What expenses have the greatest impact on profitability?
- How much revenue has been earned compared with cash collected?
- Which marketing channels generate measurable traffic and conversions?
- Are financial and operational performance improving over time?
- What trends or risks require management attention?
- Which business activities should receive additional investment or evaluation?

## Data Modeling Considerations

Because this project combines multiple reporting areas, data modeling will be an important component.

Potential analytical tables may include:

| Table | Intended Purpose |
|---|---|
| Date Dimension | Support consistent reporting periods |
| Platform Dimension | Organize marketing and content platforms |
| Content Dimension | Identify content and campaign attributes |
| Brand Dimension | Identify sponsorship partners |
| Product Dimension | Organize product and affiliate information |
| Content Performance Fact | Store content performance observations |
| Affiliate Transactions Fact | Store attributed affiliate activity |
| Sponsorship Fact | Record partnership financial and operational activity |
| Revenue Fact | Store defined revenue transactions |
| Expense Fact | Store business expenses |
| Cash Transactions Fact | Track actual cash receipts and payments |

The final model will be designed to avoid double counting, ambiguous relationships, and inconsistent aggregation.

Financial and marketing measures will be evaluated at appropriate levels of detail, and relationships between datasets will only be established where supported by reliable identifiers.

## Important Analytical Considerations

An executive dashboard should communicate business performance accurately rather than simply display a large number of metrics.

The project will prioritize:

- Consistent KPI definitions
- Clearly identified reporting periods
- Appropriate aggregation methods
- Reconciled financial totals
- Transparent attribution assumptions
- Separation of actual results and forecasts
- Documented data limitations
- Actionable business interpretation

Audience growth, engagement, and attributed revenue will not automatically be treated as evidence of causal relationships.

## Standard Project Workflow

**1. Define Executive Reporting Requirements**  
Identify stakeholders, business objectives, management questions, and reporting priorities.

**2. Identify Data Sources**  
Determine the financial, marketing, operational, and partnership datasets required.

**3. Clean & Standardize Data**  
Address missing values, inconsistent fields, duplicate records, and reporting period differences.

**4. Design the Data Model**  
Establish appropriate table relationships, identifiers, and reporting structures.

**5. Define & Calculate KPIs**  
Document metric definitions, DAX measures, accounting assumptions, and aggregation rules.

**6. Build Dashboard Visualizations**  
Create interactive executive reporting pages with clear navigation and meaningful visuals.

**7. Validate & Reconcile Results**  
Compare dashboard calculations against source records and independent control totals.

**8. Interpret Business Performance**  
Identify trends, risks, opportunities, and performance drivers supported by the data.

**9. Develop Executive Recommendations**  
Translate findings into prioritized business actions.

**10. Document & Publish**  
Prepare technical documentation, business findings, and portfolio-ready deliverables.

## Data Integrity & Professional Standards

All portfolio work will follow these principles:

- Use synthetic, public, or explicitly authorized datasets.
- Protect confidential financial, customer, creator, and partnership information.
- Document data sources, reporting periods, and usage permissions.
- Clearly define financial, operational, and marketing KPIs.
- Validate relationships between integrated datasets.
- Prevent duplicate counting of revenue, orders, and transactions.
- Distinguish revenue earned from cash collected.
- Separate actual results, estimates, and forecasts.
- Document attribution rules and limitations.
- Reconcile financial results against appropriate control totals.
- Verify DAX measures and dashboard calculations.
- Clearly distinguish guided learning projects from independently completed work.
- Avoid presenting fictional results as actual client outcomes.
