## Documentation and quality standards

Each completed project should describe the business question, data dictionary and source, grain, methodology, KPI definitions, validation, findings, limitations, and recommendations. Use **synthetic or explicitly authorized data**. Distinguish earnings from cash received; sponsorship fees from profit; and attributed conversions from proven causal impact. Do not publish real brand contracts, invoice details, audience identifiers, or private financial records without permission.
