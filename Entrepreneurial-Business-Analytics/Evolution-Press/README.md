# 8. Publishing-Specific Financial Analysis

Publishing requires careful interpretation of revenue and cost structures.

### Potential Financial Measures

| Measure | Analytical Purpose |
|---|---|
| Retail Price | Consumer-facing product price |
| Publisher Receipts | Amount received under the applicable sales arrangement |
| Royalty Income | Earnings calculated under publishing platform terms |
| Printing Cost | Production cost per physical copy |
| Distribution Fees | Costs associated with the sales channel |
| Net Sales | Sales after defined adjustments |
| Contribution Margin | Revenue less defined variable costs |
| Break-Even Volume | Units required to recover specified fixed costs |

### Important Reporting Distinction

A book with a $20 retail price does not necessarily generate $20 in revenue for the author or publisher.

The appropriate revenue measure depends on the sales arrangement.

Direct website sales and royalty-based marketplace transactions should be evaluated separately before being consolidated.

# 17. Proposed Data Model

| Table | Key Fields | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Publications | Publication ID, title, format | Publishing catalog |
| Products | Product ID, category, price | Commerce catalog |
| Sales | Transaction ID, date, channel | Sales reporting |
| Sales Items | Transaction ID, product ID, quantity | Product-level performance |
| Royalties | Publication ID, period, amount | Royalty reporting |
| Product Costs | Product ID, cost type, amount | Financial analysis |
| Discounts | Discount ID, amount | Promotional adjustments |
| Refunds | Refund ID, transaction reference | Sales adjustments |
| Downloads | Product ID, download event | Digital product activity |
| Channels | Channel ID, channel type | Distribution analysis |
| Marketing | Campaign ID, source, spending | Promotional analysis |

### Data Modeling Standards

- Use consistent product and publication identifiers.
- Avoid duplicate sales records.
- Document revenue definitions.
- Preserve transaction dates.
- Distinguish sales from royalties.
- Track refunds appropriately.
- Record pricing and cost assumptions.
- Protect confidential information.

# 21. Data Quality & Financial Integrity

### Data Quality Requirements

- Validate product identifiers.
- Review duplicate transactions.
- Confirm reporting periods.
- Reconcile sales adjustments.
- Verify product prices.
- Document cost assumptions.
- Review royalty calculations.
- Distinguish free and paid downloads.

### Financial Integrity Standards

- Do not treat retail price as royalty income.
- Do not treat revenue as profit.
- Do not confuse gross profit with contribution margin.
- Do not present projections as actual results.
- Do not assume all downloads represent sales.
- Do not combine incompatible revenue definitions.
- Do not fabricate product performance.
