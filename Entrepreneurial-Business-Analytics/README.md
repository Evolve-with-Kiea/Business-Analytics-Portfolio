# Entrepreneurial Business Analytics

## Applying Business Intelligence, Financial Analysis & Data-Driven Strategy Across an Entrepreneurial Ecosystem

### Evolve with Kiea | Business Analytics Portfolio

## Project Overview

**Entrepreneurial Business Analytics** is a structured section of my professional Business Analytics portfolio dedicated to exploring how data can support business development, financial performance, marketing effectiveness, customer relationships, and strategic decision-making.

The portfolio uses my entrepreneurial ecosystem as a foundation for developing practical analytical projects that connect academic learning, professional accounting experience, and real-world business questions.

Rather than studying analytical tools in isolation, the objective is to understand how those tools can be applied to business operations and management decisions.

Each area contains individual project frameworks that may be developed into practical analytical case studies, Excel workbooks, dashboards, SQL exercises, Python analyses, and business intelligence reports.

# 3. Business Problem

Entrepreneurs often manage several interconnected activities without having a structured way to evaluate their performance.

Sales may be tracked separately from marketing.

Website activity may be measured without connecting it to purchasing behavior.

Content performance may be evaluated using views or likes without considering broader business objectives.

Financial information may be available but not organized into useful management reports.

These challenges can make it difficult to answer fundamental questions about business performance.

### Central Business Question

**How can an entrepreneurial business ecosystem use financial, operational, marketing, customer, and digital performance data to support informed decisions, evaluate opportunities, and develop sustainable business practices?**

# 11. Analytical Methodology

Each project should follow a consistent process.

### Step 1: Define the Business Problem

Identify the decision or performance question the analysis is intended to address.

### Step 2: Establish Objectives

Translate the business problem into measurable analytical goals.

### Step 3: Identify Data Sources

Select appropriate public, synthetic, or authorized datasets.

### Step 4: Define Metrics

Document formulas, reporting periods, inclusion rules, and limitations.

### Step 5: Prepare the Data

Clean records, review data types, address duplicates, and document transformations.

### Step 6: Validate Calculations

Reconcile totals, review assumptions, and check analytical accuracy.

### Step 7: Analyze Performance

Use appropriate techniques to identify trends, patterns, and relationships.

### Step 8: Develop Visualizations

Create charts, tables, scorecards, and dashboards suited to the business question.

### Step 9: Interpret Findings

Explain what the evidence supports and identify relevant limitations.

### Step 10: Develop Recommendations

Connect findings to practical business actions.

### Step 11: Document the Project

Record methodology, tools, data sources, findings, limitations, and lessons learned.

# 17. Data Privacy & Ethical Reporting

The portfolio will prioritize responsible handling of customer, financial, marketing, and business information.

### Privacy Standards

- Use synthetic datasets for public demonstrations.
- Do not publish identifiable customer information.
- Do not disclose confidential financial records.
- Do not expose payment information.
- Do not publish restricted client data.
- Avoid revealing commercially sensitive product costs.
- Use aggregated reporting where appropriate.
- Respect platform terms and data permissions.
- Clearly label hypothetical examples.

### Important Consideration

Anonymization and pseudonymization are not equivalent.

Data should not be considered anonymous merely because names have been replaced with identifiers.
