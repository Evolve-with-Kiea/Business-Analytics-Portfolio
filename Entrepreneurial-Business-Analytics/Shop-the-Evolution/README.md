# 2. Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate information across multiple systems, including website analytics, commerce platforms, payment processors, marketing channels, and product catalogs.

However, these systems may report different metrics, apply different attribution methods, and use different transaction definitions.

A business may experience:

- Increasing website traffic without increased purchases.
- Strong order volume but declining average order value.
- High-revenue products with relatively low gross margins.
- Customer acquisition without repeat purchasing.
- Incomplete conversion tracking.
- Refunds and discounts that affect realized sales.
- Differences between sales reports and accounting records.

Without consistent definitions and analytical methods, these patterns may be difficult to interpret.

### Central Business Question

**How can Shop the Evolution™ use e-commerce, sales, product, and customer data to evaluate business performance, identify improvement opportunities, and support sustainable growth?**

# 10. Core KPI Framework

| KPI | Definition | Primary Project |
|---|---|---|
| Website Sessions | Qualifying website sessions during a period | E-commerce |
| Product-View Rate | Product-view sessions divided by eligible sessions | E-commerce |
| Add-to-Cart Rate | Eligible sessions reaching cart activity divided by the defined denominator | E-commerce |
| Purchase Conversion Rate | Purchase sessions divided by eligible sessions | E-commerce |
| Order Volume | Number of qualifying orders | Sales and Revenue |
| Gross Sales | Sales before defined reductions | Sales and Revenue |
| Net Sales | Gross sales less defined discounts and refunds | Sales and Revenue |
| Average Order Value | Defined net sales divided by qualifying orders | Sales and Revenue |
| Units Sold | Quantity of qualifying product units sold | Product Performance |
| Gross Profit | Defined revenue less cost of goods sold | Product Performance |
| Gross Margin | Gross profit divided by applicable revenue | Product Performance |
| Unit Return Rate | Returned units divided by eligible sold units | Product Performance |
| Repeat Purchase Rate | Customers with multiple qualifying purchases divided by eligible purchasing customers | Customer Analytics |
| Purchase Frequency | Qualifying orders divided by purchasing customers | Customer Analytics |
| Cohort Retention | Returning customers in a cohort divided by original cohort size | Customer Analytics |
| Estimated Customer Lifetime Value | Model-dependent estimate of future customer economic value | Customer Analytics |

### KPI Documentation Requirements

Every KPI should include:

- Business definition
- Calculation formula
- Data source
- Reporting period
- Inclusion and exclusion rules
- Relevant limitations

# 11. Financial Measurement Principles

E-commerce reporting often involves several related but distinct financial measurements.

| Measurement | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Gross Sales | Sales before specified adjustments |
| Net Sales | Sales after defined discounts and refunds |
| Recognized Revenue | Revenue recorded under applicable accounting rules |
| Gross Profit | Revenue less applicable cost of goods sold |
| Operating Profit | Profit after relevant operating expenses |
| Net Profit | Profit after all applicable expenses and other items |
| Cash Flow | Cash received and paid during a period |

### Reporting Principle

**Revenue is not the same as profit, and profit is not the same as cash flow.**

Sales reports should not automatically be presented as accounting revenue without appropriate validation.

# 13. Proposed Integrated Data Model

| Dataset | Key Fields | Analytical Application |
|---|---|---|
| Sessions | Anonymous session ID, date, channel | Website traffic |
| Events | Session ID, event type, timestamp | Conversion funnel |
| Orders | Order ID, date, status, amount | Sales reporting |
| Order Items | Order ID, product ID, quantity | Product sales |
| Products | Product ID, category, price | Product classification |
| Product Costs | Product ID, effective date, unit cost | Margin analysis |
| Discounts | Order reference, discount amount | Sales adjustments |
| Refunds | Order reference, refund date, amount | Return and refund reporting |
| Customers | Protected customer key, first purchase date | Customer analytics |
| Acquisition Channels | Channel ID, source classification | Marketing comparisons |
| Forecast Assumptions | Period, forecast inputs | Scenario analysis |

### Data Modeling Principles

- Use consistent identifiers.
- Preserve original transaction dates.
- Distinguish sessions from users.
- Distinguish orders from order items.
- Avoid duplicate sales.
- Use effective-dated product costs.
- Reconcile refunds.
- Document incomplete historical coverage.
- Protect customer confidentiality.

# 18. Attribution & Reporting Limitations

Website analytics, commerce reporting, and marketing platforms may assign conversions differently.

### Illustrative Journey

**Pinterest → Product Page → Email Signup → Return Website Visit → Purchase**

Different reporting systems may attribute the resulting purchase to different sources.

### Potential Limitations

- Multiple marketing touchpoints
- Cross-session purchases
- Cross-device activity
- Attribution window differences
- Incomplete event tracking
- Privacy-related restrictions
- Differences between analytics and commerce records

### Reporting Standard

Attribution should be presented as a documented reporting method, not proof that one channel caused a purchase.

---

# 19. Privacy & Ethical Data Practices

This portfolio will prioritize responsible handling of customer and business information.

### Data Protection Standards

- Use synthetic datasets for public demonstrations.
- Do not publish customer names.
- Do not disclose customer email addresses.
- Do not expose shipping or billing addresses.
- Do not publish payment information.
- Do not disclose identifiable customer purchasing histories.
- Avoid publishing confidential supplier or cost records.
- Use aggregated reporting where appropriate.
- Document data permissions.
- Respect applicable platform terms.

### Important Consideration

Pseudonymous customer identifiers may still be personal data if they can be linked back to individuals.

Public datasets should be fully synthetic or otherwise appropriately protected and authorized.
