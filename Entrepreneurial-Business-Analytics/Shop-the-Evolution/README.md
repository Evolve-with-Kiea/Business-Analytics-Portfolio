# Shop the Evolution Analytics

Potential analytics projects for the e-commerce and product ecosystem:

- [E-commerce Analytics](E-commerce-Analytics/README.md) — traffic, conversion, sessions, cart activity, and storefront performance.
- [Sales and Revenue](Sales-and-Revenue/README.md) — orders, revenue trends, average order value, discounts, and refunds.
- [Product Performance](Product-Performance/README.md) — product sales, margins, demand, and assortment performance.
- [Customer Analytics](Customer-Analytics/README.md) — segments, repeat purchasing, retention, and lifetime-value concepts.

Use synthetic or anonymized data for demonstrations. Never publish identifiable customer or confidential store records.
