# Brand Evolution Marketing Agency Analytics

## Marketing Intelligence, Client Performance, Financial Analytics & Startup Business Growth

### Overview

**Brand Evolution Marketing Agency Analytics** is a dedicated section of my Business Analytics portfolio focused on applying analytical methods to marketing services, campaign measurement, client reporting, financial performance, and the development of a professional services business.

The section is organized around **Brand Evolution Marketing Agency**, an emerging business initiative within the broader Evolve with Kiea ecosystem.

Its purpose is to explore how marketing, accounting, financial analysis, and business intelligence can work together to support better decisions for entrepreneurs, small businesses, service providers, and product-based organizations.

The projects in this section are designed to demonstrate how an analyst can move beyond reporting marketing activity to evaluating business outcomes, financial sustainability, operational efficiency, and opportunities for improvement.

As I pursue my Associate of Applied Science in Business Analytics at Wake Technical Community College, this section will provide a structured environment for developing practical analytical skills using Excel, SQL, Python, Power BI, and related business tools.

My professional background in accounting and financial operations provides an additional foundation for analyzing marketing costs, revenue, cash flow, profitability, and the financial implications of business decisions.

**Current Status:** Portfolio project frameworks established. Individual analytical projects remain planned and should not be represented as completed until supporting work has been developed and validated.

# 11. Common Analytical Standards

All projects in this section should follow consistent documentation and quality requirements.

### Business Understanding

- Define the business problem.
- Identify the intended audience.
- Establish measurable objectives.
- Select relevant KPIs.
- Explain the business significance of the analysis.

### Data Quality

- Document data sources.
- Review missing values.
- Investigate duplicates.
- Confirm reporting periods.
- Validate calculations.
- Identify data limitations.

### Financial Integrity

- Define cost categories.
- Distinguish revenue and profit.
- Identify accounting assumptions.
- Reconcile financial data when possible.
- Avoid unsupported financial conclusions.

### Reporting Quality

- Use clear visualizations.
- Provide understandable explanations.
- Connect findings to evidence.
- Identify limitations.
- Recommend practical next actions.

### Portfolio Integrity

- Label synthetic data.
- Distinguish planned work from completed work.
- Record demonstrated skills only when supported.
- Avoid fabricated business results.
- Maintain accurate project statuses.

# 15. Data Security, Client Confidentiality & Ethical Reporting

Professional analytics work requires careful handling of confidential information.

### Acceptable Portfolio Data

- Fictional client scenarios
- Synthetic datasets
- Public datasets with appropriate usage permissions
- Authorized business information suitable for publication
- Appropriately anonymized data where publication is permitted

### Restricted Information

Do not publish:

- Client-confidential information
- Customer personal information
- Private financial statements
- Confidential contracts
- Advertising account credentials
- API keys
- Proprietary campaign strategies
- Unapproved client performance results
- Identifiable business information without appropriate authorization

### Client Permission Requirements

Explicit permission is required before publishing identifiable client information or nonpublic client results.

Anonymization does not automatically eliminate confidentiality or contractual restrictions.

### Professional Integrity

**No project should imply that Brand Evolution Marketing Agency has delivered client services, produced measurable client results, or completed technical work without supporting evidence.**
