# Business Case Studies

A case study should keep its business question, analysis, evidence, and recommendations together in one project folder. Do not split one case across separate Business Problems, Data Analysis, and Recommendations directories.

## Current example

The [AR Collections project](../Excel-Projects/Financial-Analysis/AR-Collections-Analysis/README.md) demonstrates a business question, supporting invoice analysis, and collection recommendations. It remains in its Excel folder; this index does not create a second copy or count it as another project.

## Case study outline

1. Define the decision and intended audience.
2. Describe the data and its limitations.
3. Show the methods, checks, and findings.
4. Recommend actions supported by those findings.
5. Explain uncertainty and what to investigate next.
