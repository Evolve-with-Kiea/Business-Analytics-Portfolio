# Financial Reporting

## Purpose

This folder is for projects that analyze the three main financial statements and interpret overall financial performance.

> **Educational purpose.** Analyses in this section are learning exercises that use public or synthetic data. They are not professional accounting, tax, investment, or financial advice, and they should not be used to make financial decisions about a real business or person.

## Topics

| Topic | What to analyze |
|---|---|
| Income statement analysis | Revenue, expenses, and profit over a period |
| Balance sheet analysis | Assets, liabilities, and equity at a point in time |
| Cash flow analysis | Operating, investing, and financing cash movements |
| Financial ratios | Liquidity, profitability, leverage, and efficiency |
| Performance interpretation | What the numbers together suggest and what they leave out |


## Example Ratio Families

| Family | Question it addresses |
|---|---|
| Liquidity | Can the business meet short-term obligations? |
| Profitability | How effectively does the business turn revenue into profit? |
| Leverage | How much does the business rely on debt? |
| Efficiency | How well does the business use its assets? |


## Data Notes

Public company filings are available through the [SEC EDGAR database](https://www.sec.gov/edgar). Cite the specific filing and period used, and avoid drawing conclusions about investment decisions.
