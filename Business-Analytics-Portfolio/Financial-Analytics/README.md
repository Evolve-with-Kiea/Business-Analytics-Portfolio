# Financial Analytics

This section will hold educational analyses of revenue, profitability, expenses, and financial statements using public or synthetic data.

## Project Areas

| Folder | Focus |
|---|---|
| [Revenue-and-Profitability](Revenue-and-Profitability/README.md) | Revenue trends, margins, and break-even analysis |
| [Expense-Analysis](Expense-Analysis/README.md) | Cost behavior, budgets, and cost control |
| [Financial-Reporting](Financial-Reporting/README.md) | Statement analysis, ratios, and interpretation |

## What This Section Will Demonstrate

- Understanding of how financial statements connect
- Calculation and interpretation of key financial measures
- Clear separation of facts, assumptions, and conclusions
- Recognition of the limits of the data

> **Educational purpose.** Analyses in this section are learning exercises that use public or synthetic data. They are not professional accounting, tax, investment, or financial advice, and they should not be used to make financial decisions about a real business or person.

## How Projects Are Added

1. Choose a business question and note it in the project README.
2. Select a public, synthetic, or anonymized dataset (see [Dataset Sources](../Documentation/Dataset-Sources.md)).
3. Follow the [Analytical Methodology](../Documentation/Analytical-Methodology.md).
4. Document the project using the [Project Documentation Standards](../Documentation/Project-Documentation-Standards.md).
5. Update [Portfolio Progress](../Documentation/Portfolio-Progress.md).
