# Recommendations

## Purpose

This folder is for the final stage of a case study: turning findings into implications, recommendations, and an honest statement of limitations.

## Template

```markdown
## Business Implications
What do the findings mean for the business?

## Recommendations
| Recommendation | Supporting finding | Expected benefit | Risks or considerations |
|---|---|---|---|
|  |  |  |  |

## Limitations
- Data limitations:
- Assumptions:
- Scope limits:

## Conclusion
Summarize the answer to the business problem and suggest next steps.
```

## Recommendation Standards

- Each recommendation is tied to a specific finding.
- Recommendations are actionable and proportionate to the evidence.
- Risks and alternatives are noted.
- Benefits are described as expectations, not guarantees.

## Reminder

Case studies are educational. Recommendations based on public or synthetic data illustrate analytical thinking and should not be presented as advice for a real business without appropriate data and professional review.

## Potential Project Ideas

These are ideas for future work. They are not completed projects.

- Write recommendations for a completed practice case study
- Prepare a one-page executive summary of a case study
