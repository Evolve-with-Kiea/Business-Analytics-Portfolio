# Business Problems

## Purpose

This folder is for the first stage of a case study: defining the business problem clearly before any analysis begins. Starting with the problem keeps the work focused on a decision rather than on a tool.

## Template

Copy the following into a new file for each case study.

```markdown
# [Case Study Title]

## Business Problem
Describe the issue in one or two sentences.

## Background
Provide context: the type of business, the situation, and why it matters.
State whether the business is real, hypothetical, or based on public data.

## Business Objectives
- Objective 1 (specific and measurable where possible)
- Objective 2

## Research Questions
1. Question the analysis will answer
2. Question the analysis will answer

## Success Criteria
How will I know the analysis answered the question?
```

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] The problem is specific, not general
- [ ] Objectives connect to a decision
- [ ] Research questions can be answered with available data
- [ ] The scenario is labeled real, hypothetical, or public-data-based
- [ ] No confidential or private information is included

## Examples of Problem Types

- A product line's profitability is unclear.
- Marketing spend is rising without clear return.
- A workflow causes repeated delays.
- A business wants to know which customers return.
