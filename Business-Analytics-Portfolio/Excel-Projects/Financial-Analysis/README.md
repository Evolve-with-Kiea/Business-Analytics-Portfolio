# Excel Financial Analysis

## Purpose

This folder is for Excel analyses of financial data, such as income statements, budgets, and ratios, using public or synthetic data.

> **Educational purpose.** Analyses in this section are learning exercises that use public or synthetic data. They are not professional accounting, tax, investment, or financial advice, and they should not be used to make financial decisions about a real business or person.

## Analysis Areas

| Area | Content |
|---|---|
| Revenue analysis | Revenue by product, period, or segment |
| Expense analysis | Expense categories and trends |
| Gross profit | Revenue minus cost of goods sold |
| Operating profit | Gross profit minus operating expenses |
| Net profit | Profit after all expenses, interest, and taxes |
| Profit margins | Gross, operating, and net margin percentages |
| Budget versus actual | Variances between plan and results |
| Financial ratios | Liquidity, profitability, and efficiency ratios |
| Financial trends | Multi-period comparisons |


## Suggested Excel Techniques

- Formulas for margins, variances, and growth rates
- SUMIFS for category totals
- PivotTables for period and category summaries
- Charts for trend and composition
- Conditional formatting for favorable and unfavorable variances
- Data validation and checks that totals reconcile

## Interpretation Expectations

Each analysis should explain what a margin, variance, or ratio means, why it changed, and what limitations apply, such as a small sample or missing context.
