# Business Analytics Portfolio

This repository is my professional portfolio for applied business analytics. It is designed to show how I use analytical concepts and technical tools to approach realistic business problems.

**Repository:** [github.com/EvolvewithKiea/Business-Analytics-Portfolio](https://github.com/EvolvewithKiea/Business-Analytics-Portfolio)

## Portfolio Status

This is a **developing portfolio**. I am building my capabilities through academic coursework, independent learning, certifications, and practical exercises. I am not presenting myself as an experienced data analyst, business intelligence professional, or financial analyst.

Projects are added only after I complete them, and every project documents its data, methods, and limitations. At this stage, the repository contains the structure and documentation standards that future projects will follow. **No projects have been completed yet.** Progress is recorded in [Portfolio Progress](Documentation/Portfolio-Progress.md).

## Professional Objective

I want to understand business problems, analyze relevant information, identify patterns and opportunities, and communicate findings that support informed decisions. I am especially interested in connecting business knowledge with technical analysis, so that every tool I use serves a clear business question.

## Data Security and Ethical Standards

All projects use one of the following:

- Public datasets
- Synthetic datasets
- Appropriately anonymized data

This repository must **never** contain:

- Confidential employer information
- Private client information
- Customer personal information
- Passwords
- API keys
- Private financial records
- Proprietary business data

Project demonstrations must respect applicable privacy, licensing, and data-use requirements. Details are in [Project Documentation Standards](Documentation/Project-Documentation-Standards.md) and [Dataset Sources](Documentation/Dataset-Sources.md).
