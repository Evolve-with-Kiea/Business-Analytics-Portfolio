# Marketing Analytics

This section will hold projects that use data to evaluate marketing performance, customer behavior, and return on marketing spend. Analytical findings can support marketing decisions by showing which activities, channels, and customer groups contribute most to results, and by identifying where more testing or information is needed.

## Project Areas

| Folder | Focus |
|---|---|
| [Campaign-Performance](Campaign-Performance/README.md) | Reach, engagement, and conversion measures |
| [Customer-Analysis](Customer-Analysis/README.md) | Acquisition, retention, value, and segmentation |
| [Marketing-ROI](Marketing-ROI/README.md) | Spend, attributed revenue, and channel profitability |

## What This Section Will Demonstrate

- Selection and interpretation of marketing measures
- Comparison of campaigns and channels
- Understanding of customer behavior patterns
- Awareness of attribution limits

## How Projects Are Added

1. Choose a business question and note it in the project README.
2. Select a public, synthetic, or anonymized dataset (see [Dataset Sources](../Documentation/Dataset-Sources.md)).
3. Follow the [Analytical Methodology](../Documentation/Analytical-Methodology.md).
4. Document the project using the [Project Documentation Standards](../Documentation/Project-Documentation-Standards.md).
5. Update [Portfolio Progress](../Documentation/Portfolio-Progress.md).
