# Campaign Performance

## Purpose

This folder is for projects that measure how marketing campaigns perform and compare.

## Measures

| Measure | Definition | Calculation method |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions | Number of times content was displayed | Reported by the platform |
| Reach | Number of unique people who saw the content | Reported by the platform |
| Click-through rate | Share of impressions that resulted in clicks | Clicks divided by impressions |
| Conversion rate | Share of visitors who completed a desired action | Conversions divided by visitors or clicks |
| Cost per click | Average cost of each click | Spend divided by clicks |
| Cost per acquisition | Average cost of each conversion | Spend divided by conversions |


## Campaign Comparisons

Compare campaigns using consistent time periods, similar audiences where possible, and the same definitions. Note differences in budget, targeting, and creative that could explain results.

## How Findings Support Decisions

- Identify campaigns that deliver conversions at lower cost.
- Spot stages where audiences drop off.
- Suggest tests, such as changing a message or audience.
