# Marketing ROI

## Purpose

This folder is for projects that compare marketing spending with the results it appears to produce.

## Topics

| Topic | What to analyze |
|---|---|
| Marketing expenditure | Total and category spending |
| Revenue attributed to campaigns | Sales linked to campaigns under a stated attribution method |
| Return on marketing investment | Return relative to spend |
| Campaign profitability | Whether a campaign's contribution exceeded its cost |
| Channel performance | Comparison of channels by cost and return |


## Calculation Reference

| Measure | Calculation method |
|---|---|
| Return on marketing investment | (Attributed revenue minus marketing cost) divided by marketing cost |
| Return on ad spend | Attributed revenue divided by ad spend |
| Campaign profit | Attributed gross profit minus campaign cost |


## Attribution Caution

Attribution methods are assumptions. Last-click credit, first-click credit, and other models can give different answers. State which model is used, and avoid claiming that a campaign caused a sale unless the design supports it.
